## Arriving
[softly] Welcome. For the next ten minutes, there is nowhere else you need to be. Find a way of sitting or lying that lets your body rest. Let your hands settle wherever they are comfortable.

Let your eyes close, or let your gaze rest softly on the floor in front of you.

Take a slow breath in... and a long breath out. And again, in... and out, letting the shoulders drop.

[[silence 5s]]

Most of us carry more than we know. Things that happened. Things that were said. Things we are still waiting for, or still afraid of. We carry them so long that we forget we picked them up. They start to feel like part of us.

They aren't. They are just things you have been holding. And for these few minutes, you might find you can set some of them down.

## Finding where it is held
[warmly] Bring to mind something you have been carrying. It doesn't need to be the biggest thing. Something that has been sitting with you lately. Just let it come into view, without going over it again.

[[silence 10s]]

Now notice where you feel it in your body. It might be a tightness in the chest. A knot in the stomach. A weight on the shoulders. A clenching in the jaw. There is no right answer. Just notice where it lives.

[[silence 15s]]

Breathe into that place. Not to push it away. Just to keep it company. Let the breath reach it, gently, the way you might rest a warm hand on someone who is hurting.

[[silence 20s]]

## The river
[softly] [slowly] Now imagine you are sitting on the bank of a slow river. The water is clear and unhurried. It moves around the stones without fighting them. It has been flowing like this for a very long time.

In your hands, you are holding the thing you've been carrying. Feel its weight. Notice its shape. You have been holding it so carefully, for so long.

[[silence 10s]]

When you are ready, and only if you are ready, lean forward and place it on the surface of the water. You don't need to throw it. Just let it go.

[[silence 15s]]

Watch the river take it. Slowly. Carefully. It turns once in the current... and moves away downstream. A little further now. Smaller. And smaller. Until you can hardly see it at all.

[[silence 20s]]

You might find it comes back. That's alright. Things we have carried for a long time often do. Each time it does, you can set it on the water again. Letting go is rarely one moment. It is something we practise.

## The space that is left
[softly] Now notice your hands. Empty. Resting. Notice the place in your body where the weight was. Is there a little more room there now? Even a little?

[[silence 20s]]

Rest in that space. Let the breath move freely through it. You don't need to fill it with anything. Just let it be open for a while.

[[silence 40s]]

You are not what happened to you. You are not the thing you have been carrying. You are the one who has been carrying it, and you can rest.

[[silence 20s]]

## Carrying something lighter
[warmly] As we come towards the end, think of one small, kind thing you could carry into the rest of today instead. A slower breath when things get busy. A softer word to yourself. A moment of looking out of the window.

[[silence 10s]]

Take one more full breath in... and let it go.

[[silence 5s]]

Thank you for letting yourself set something down today. If it comes back tomorrow, that's alright. Come back, and we'll sit by the river together again.
