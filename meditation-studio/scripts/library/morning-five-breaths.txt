## Opening
[softly] Good morning. Before the day gets its hands on you, let's take a few minutes that belong only to you.

You don't need to sit any special way. Sitting up in bed is fine. A chair by the window is fine. Just let your back be tall enough to breathe, and let your hands rest wherever they want to rest.

Notice the weight of your body. The places where you are held... by the bed, by the chair, by the floor. You don't have to hold yourself up right now. Something already is.

[[silence 10s]]

## Teaching
[warmly] Most of us wake up and go straight into the day. The phone, the list, the worry that was waiting by the bed. We tense up before we've even opened our eyes properly. And we call that being ready.

But holding on that tightly doesn't keep you safe. It just keeps you tired. One slow breath, taken on purpose, is a way of putting all of that down... even for a moment.

So this morning, we're going to take five. That's all. Five slow breaths are plenty. The practice is simply remembering to take them.

## Practice
[softly] Let's begin. Breathe in through your nose... and let it go, slowly, through your mouth. That's one.

[[silence 10s]]

Breathe in again, and this time feel where the breath goes. The chest, the belly, the back. And let it go. That's two.

[[silence 10s]]

Three. Breathe in... and as you breathe out, let the shoulders drop. They've been up near your ears for longer than you know.

[[silence 10s]]

Four. In... and out. If your mind has already wandered off into the day, that's alright. Losing count doesn't matter. Noticing that you've wandered, and coming back, is the whole practice.

[[silence 10s]]

[slowly] And five. A full breath in... hold it for just a moment at the top... and let it all go.

[[silence 10s]]

Now let the breath breathe itself for a little while. Nothing to count. Nothing to do. Just this.

[[silence 30s]]

## Gratitude
[warmly] Now, think of one ordinary thing you are glad of this morning. Not a big thing. The warmth of the duvet. The kettle. A voice you'll hear later. Just one.

Let yourself actually feel it, for a breath or two. You don't have to make it into anything.

[[silence 25s]]

## Integration
[softly] Here is the one thing to carry into today. At some point, something will rub you up the wrong way. An email, a queue, a tone of voice. When it happens, take one breath before you react. Just one. That breath is you coming back.

You might forget. Most days, I do too. When you notice you've forgotten, that's the moment. Take the breath then.

[[silence 20s]]

## Closing
[warmly] Thank you for giving yourself these few minutes. They count for more than you think.

I'll be here tomorrow morning, at the same quiet place in the day, if you'd like to take your five breaths together again.

Go gently into your day.
