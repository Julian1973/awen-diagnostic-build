## Arriving
[softly] This is five minutes for you. Wherever you are, just pause. You don't need to change anything about how you're sitting, or how you feel.

Take one slow breath in... and let it out with a sigh.

## A hand on the heart
[warmly] If it feels right, place one hand on the middle of your chest. Or on your cheek, or over your other hand. Feel the warmth of your own touch. Notice the gentle pressure of it.

You have probably done this for other people a thousand times. A hand on a shoulder. A hand on a back. Now you are offering it to yourself.

[[silence 5s]]

## Naming what is hard
[softly] Let yourself notice what is hard right now. You don't need to explain it, or solve it. Just quietly name it to yourself. This is hard. Or, I'm tired. Or, I'm struggling today.

[[silence 8s]]

Whatever you are feeling, you are not the only one. Somewhere, right now, someone else is feeling exactly this. Struggling is part of being human. It doesn't mean you have done anything wrong.

## Kind words
[warmly] Now, imagine what you would say to a good friend who felt the way you do now. You would probably be gentle. Patient. You might tell them it's alright to find this hard. That they are doing their best. That they don't have to carry it all alone.

[[silence 8s]]

See if you can say some of those words to yourself. Quietly, in your own way. It might be, I'm here for you. Or, it's alright, I've got you. Or simply, may I be kind to myself.

[[silence 15s]]

If it feels strange, or untrue, that's alright. Kindness to ourselves is often the last kindness we learn. Just let the words be there, even if you don't believe them yet.

[[silence 10s]]

## Rest
[softly] Feel the warmth of your hand once more. Breathe in... and breathe out.

[[silence 8s]]

When you're ready, let your hand rest and let your eyes open. Take a little of this kindness with you into the rest of your day.

Come back tomorrow, and we'll take another five minutes together.
