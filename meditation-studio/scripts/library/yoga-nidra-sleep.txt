## Settling
[softly] This is a time to rest. You don't need to do anything well tonight. Just lie down, let your head be supported, and let your arms rest a little away from your sides, palms open if that feels easy.

Let your eyes close when they are ready. Let the bed take your weight. The back of the head, the shoulders, the backs of the legs, the heels. All of it held.

Take a slow breath in... and let it go through the mouth. Once more, in... and a long, easy breath out.

[[silence 8s]]

You might hear sounds around you. A car outside, the house settling, your own breathing. Let them be there. Nothing needs to be shut out. Sounds can come and go, and you can stay right here.

This practice is called yoga nidra. All you do is listen to my voice and let your attention go where it is invited. If you drift off, that's perfect. If you stay awake, that's perfect too. There is no way to get this wrong.

Let the face soften. Let the jaw be loose, the tongue resting. Let the shoulders drop away from the ears. If anything feels uncomfortable, move now, so that you can be still. Then let the body settle, the way snow settles after it has been shaken.

## The wish
[warmly] Before we begin, let a simple wish come to mind. Something true and kind, said as if it is already so. It might be, I am allowed to rest. Or, I can let the day go now. Or a few words of your own.

Say it quietly to yourself, three times.

[[silence 12s]]

And now let it go. It has been heard.

## Travelling through the body
[softly] [slowly] Now we travel through the body. As I name each place, simply notice it. You don't need to move it or change it. Just let your attention rest there for a moment, the way a hand might rest on a shoulder.

Right hand. The thumb. First finger. Second finger. Third finger. Little finger. The palm of the hand. The back of the hand. The wrist. The forearm. The elbow. The upper arm. The shoulder. The armpit. The right side of the waist. The right hip. The thigh. The knee. The shin. The ankle. The heel. The sole of the foot. The top of the foot. The big toe. Second toe. Third toe. Fourth toe. Little toe.

Left hand. The thumb. First finger. Second finger. Third finger. Little finger. The palm of the hand. The back of the hand. The wrist. The forearm. The elbow. The upper arm. The shoulder. The armpit. The left side of the waist. The left hip. The thigh. The knee. The shin. The ankle. The heel. The sole of the foot. The top of the foot. The big toe. Second toe. Third toe. Fourth toe. Little toe.

Now the back of the body. The back of the head, resting. The right shoulder blade. The left shoulder blade. The whole of the upper back. The lower back, softening. The backs of both legs. Both heels, heavy.

And the front of the body. The top of the head. The forehead, smooth. The eyebrows. The eyelids, heavy. The right cheek. The left cheek. The nose, and the cool air moving through it. The upper lip. The lower lip. The jaw, loose. The chin. The throat. The right side of the chest. The left side of the chest. The middle of the chest. The belly, rising and falling. The lower belly.

The whole right leg. The whole left leg. Both legs together. The whole right arm. The whole left arm. Both arms together. The whole of the back. The whole of the front. The whole body, together.

Feel the body as one shape, lying here. The outline of it against the bed. The space it takes up in the room. The whole body, resting.

[[silence 20s]]

## Breath
[softly] Now notice the breath, just as it is. You don't need to breathe in any special way. Let the body breathe itself, the way it does all night without you.

Feel the cool air coming in at the nostrils... and the slightly warmer air leaving.

Now begin to count the breaths backwards, from twenty seven down to one. Breathing in, twenty seven. Breathing out, twenty seven. Breathing in, twenty six. Breathing out, twenty six. Carry on by yourself now, in your own time.

If you lose count, that's welcome. Losing count is part of letting go. Simply start again from twenty seven, or let the counting fall away altogether.

[[silence 90s]]

Let the counting go now. Let the breath be.

[[silence 10s]]

## Opposites
[slowly] Now bring to mind the feeling of heaviness. The whole body heavy. Heavy as a stone at the bottom of a still pool. The arms heavy. The legs heavy. Sinking into the bed.

[[silence 15s]]

And now lightness. The body light as a feather. So light it might drift up from the bed. The arms light. The legs light. The whole body weightless.

[[silence 15s]]

Heaviness again... and lightness... Let them come and go, one after the other.

[[silence 10s]]

Now warmth. The warmth of a summer afternoon on your skin. Warmth spreading through the hands, the feet, the belly. Warm right through.

[[silence 15s]]

And now coolness. Cool air on a winter morning. The coolness of a stream running over your hands. Cool and fresh.

[[silence 15s]]

Warmth again... and coolness... and let them both go.

[[silence 15s]]

## Pictures
[softly] [slowly] Now let some pictures pass through your mind. You don't need to hold on to them. Let each one come... and go.

A candle flame, quite still... A harbour at night, boats resting on dark water...

Rain on a slate roof... A lamp in a farmhouse window...

A gull resting on the sea... Bread cooling on a kitchen table...

A path through a wood, and moss on the stones... Smoke rising slowly from a chimney...

The first star... A hand around a warm cup...

Snow falling on the hills... The sea, breathing.

[[silence 40s]]

## The wish again
[softly] [slowly] Now remember your simple wish from the beginning. Say it once more, quietly, as if it is already true.

[[silence 15s]]

## Drifting
[softly] [slowly] There is nothing more to do now. Nowhere to go. Nothing to fix before morning. The night can hold you.

Whatever happened today is finished for now. Whatever tomorrow brings can wait for tomorrow. Tonight, you are allowed to rest.

If sleep is close, let it come. You don't have to make it happen. It knows the way.

[[silence 60s]]

Let the body be heavy... and the mind be quiet... and let go.

[[silence 90s]]

Resting... safe... held.
