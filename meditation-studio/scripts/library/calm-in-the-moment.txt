## Arriving
[softly] If you've come here because your mind is racing, or your chest is tight, or worry won't leave you alone, you are in the right place. You don't need to calm down before we begin. Come exactly as you are.

Let's start by simply noticing your feet. Wherever you are, feel them. Pressing into the floor, or resting on the bed. Feel the weight of them. Feel the ground underneath them, solid and steady.

[[silence 6s]]

## Naming it
[warmly] Anxiety is a feeling of the body getting ready for danger. Your heart might be quicker. Your breath might be high in your chest. Your thoughts might be running ahead into everything that could go wrong.

None of this means something is wrong with you. It is your body trying very hard to keep you safe. We are going to let it know, gently, that right now, in this moment, it can stand down.

Quietly say to yourself, this is anxiety. It is uncomfortable, and it will pass.

[[silence 8s]]

## Grounding
[softly] [slowly] Now let your attention come down into the body. Feel where you are sitting or lying. Feel the chair or the bed holding your weight. You don't have to hold yourself up right now.

Notice five things you can hear. Near sounds and far sounds. The hum of a room. Traffic outside. Your own breathing. Don't go looking. Just let the sounds arrive.

[[silence 15s]]

Now notice what you can feel against your skin. The fabric of your clothes. The air on your face and hands. The temperature of the room.

[[silence 12s]]

Notice any smell in the room, or the taste in your mouth. Small, ordinary things. They are all happening now, in this moment, not in the future your mind keeps running towards.

[[silence 10s]]

And notice the feet again. Still there. Still on solid ground.

## Long breaths out
[softly] [slowly] Now we'll slow the breath, without forcing it. Breathe in gently through your nose for a count of four. One, two, three, four. And breathe out slowly through your mouth for a count of six. One, two, three, four, five, six.

Again. In, two, three, four. And out, two, three, four, five, six.

A long breath out is one of the clearest messages you can send your body that it is safe. Keep going in your own time. In for four... out for six.

[[silence 55s]]

If your mind pulls you back to the worry, that's alright. It will. Just notice it, and come back to the feet, and the long breath out.

[[silence 20s]]

## Allowing
[softly] Now see if you can find where the anxiety sits in your body. Perhaps the chest, or the stomach, or the throat. Instead of fighting it, let it be there for a moment. Breathe around it. Give it a little room.

[[silence 15s]]

You might notice it changes when you stop pushing against it. It might soften, or move, or stay just as it is. All of that is fine. You are not trying to get rid of anything. You are just letting it be, and staying with yourself.

[[silence 20s]]

## A kind word
[warmly] Place a hand on your chest, or your belly, if that feels comforting. And say to yourself, I'm safe enough, right now. Or, I can handle this moment. Or, it's alright, I'm here.

[[silence 12s]]

## One small step
[softly] Before we finish, think of one small, simple thing you can do next. A glass of water. Opening a window. Stepping outside for a minute. Sending a message to someone you trust. Just one small step.

[[silence 8s]]

Feel your feet one last time, on solid ground. Take one more slow breath in... and a long breath out.

[[silence 6s]]

Well done for staying with yourself. Whenever worry comes back, and it may, you can come back here. Tomorrow, or any time you need to find solid ground again.
