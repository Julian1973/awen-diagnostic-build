Scene Delivery Loop

Two decisions before a scene starts, five steps per shot, and one rule about order. Everything else in the pipeline is machinery that enforces this.

Scene zero once per scene · ~5 min

The half-day Scene 1 lost was spent here — after the fact, at approval, three takes deep.

  1. 01

    Cast

    List everyone the scene puts on screen. Each is exactly one of two things. Named cast — the audience must recognise them; they speak, act or recur — needs a locked single-subject pack and its own reference slot on every shot they appear in. Unnamed presence — background, no line, never recognised again — stays out of the cast and is never named in the prompt. There is no third state: a named character without a pack is a stranger waiting to be invented.

    Done whencb_canon.py status Ep1 reports stubs: none for everyone this scene stages.

  2. 02

    Split

    One dramatic change per shot. Maximum three causal stages and three motivated views; 6–15 seconds for character work. Thirty seconds is a ceiling, not a target. A shot carrying a read, a wrong word, an embarrassment, an entrance, a line and a retry is six shots wearing one shot's clothes — and it will cost four takes to find that out.

    Done whenYou can say what changes, in one sentence, for every shot in the scene.

Every shot repeats · the studio's four stages

  1. 03

    Direct

    The beat, the blocking, the landing frame. Name the opening state, the single change, and the exact image the next shot inherits.

    Done whenThe landing frame is written precisely enough to open the following shot.

  2. 04

    See

    The keyframe. You generate it from the prompt written for you — that is what buys 100% accuracy on composition. Compose motion room into it: empty space in the direction the movement goes.

    Done whenThe frame is approved and every face that carries the scene is bigger than a tenth of frame.

  3. 05

    Hear

    The ElevenLabs performance. It is the authority for words, cadence, delivery, mouth timing and silence — the prompt never invents a line and never re-times one.

    Done whenThe take is approved and mapped to the correct speaker.

  4. 06

    Watch

    Compile the prompt, score it, clear the gates, fire. Draft at 720p while hunting composition; spend 4K once the shot is locked.

    Done whenThe audit scores 9.5+ on the current text and no gate refuses.

  5. 07

    Judge

    Approve, or reject with the reason. The reason is the correction — it becomes the next fire's direction. Reject at the first failed gate rather than accepting a compromised take and compounding it.

    Done whenThe closing state is recorded. The record, not memory, opens the next shot.

The one rule about order

cast → direction → fire → approve

Cast is an input to the prompt compile. Clear a stale direction before settling the cast and it goes stale again the moment the cast changes. Reverse any two of these and the work is done twice.

When it refuses

A refusal is the pipeline naming a fault it can still fix for free. Read the code, go back to the step it belongs to.

RefusalWhat it means, and where to go
CAST_UNREFERENCED
CAST CANON INCOMPLETE		Someone on screen has nothing to be drawn from. Back to step 01 — lock the pack, or declare the role an unnamed presence.
direction is staleThe direction changed under the stored prompt; the take you are approving came from a prompt that no longer exists. Re-prepare direction, then re-fire — never approve through it.
still says Approve
after you approved		Not a UI glitch and not a lost click. The stage was approved, then an upstream change moved its input signature, so it stopped counting as current. Approving again cannot fix it — there is nothing wrong with the approval. Re-prepare that stage, then approve the fresh candidate.
AUDIT_MISSING
AUDIT_INVALIDATED		The text moved after it was scored. Re-score the current words; a correction is a new prompt and needs its own round.
WRAPPER_OVERLOADEDA beat is carrying a scene. Back to step 02 — split it.
SPEAKER_BOX_REQUIREDTwo faces and one line, and the sync route cannot choose. Box the speaker before the sync pass.
KEYFRAME_UNDERSPECIFIEDA keyframe with no named frame, no immutable facts, or an ensemble with unbound characters. Name them.
EXTENSION_UNDERSPECIFIEDContinuing footage without an approved master, already-true facts, or 2–3 identity anchors. Fill them in — and never extend a drifting clip.

The three questions

Q1

Who is in this frame, and does every one of them have a reference?

Q2

What single thing changes?

Q3

What is the last frame, and can the next shot start from it?

Five standards, two skills and thirteen gates exist to enforce these three answers. You never need to read any of it to produce — it needs reading only when something refuses, and the refusal tells you which page.
