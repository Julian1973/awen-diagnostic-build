Then: inside the box the brass cylinder begins to rotate steadily, its raised pins passing across the teeth of the steel comb, the individual teeth flicking as the pins release them.

End state: both hands withdrawn and resting flat on the cloth, motionless. The cylinder still turning inside the open box. Nothing else in the frame moves.

Only two hands are in this frame. No face and no other person appears at any point. The box, the cloth, the counter and the lamplight stay exactly as they are.
