## WHAT THE AUDIT SHOWS

**The first four minutes are ready to shoot** once the keyframes exist. Sets
are complete, all 24 lines are recorded, the route is chosen.

**Act Two is the real work** — eleven shots, no plates, no dialogue, in a
period and a country we have never built. It is also 5 of the 11 minutes and
contains the episode's turn (the compass waking on Peggi's question).

**Three characters are underserved for their importance:**
- **Pascal** carries Act Two's whole emotional load with no cadence card.
- **Macsen** performs in the first shot, on the counter in Act One, and watches
  Tom leave in Act Three — pack exists, but no bookshop-window set.
- **Mrs Patel** closes the episode and has no design.

**The order I would work in:** 7 keyframes for cold open + Act One → fire those
9 shots → judge → then Act Two plates, because Act Two is where the money goes
and it should be spent after the pipeline has proven itself on a scene we can
already fully build.
