# MP.B1 — "The Object Arrives" · Director record
Movement proof, beat 1 of 5 · The Wrong Tune · workshop interior (Inside Shop plate)
Status: DIRECTED — compiles when TP-01 (broken box) and TP-03 (keyframe) are approved.

## Purpose
The laughs land before the ache: ordinary family noise, then a broken thing
arrives and the room learns to listen. Establishes the series ritual —
Richard's apron-moment — without one word of explanation.

## Engine
Contrast cut in miniature: bustle → stillness. The wrong tune itself is the
punchline AND the wound: the box plays three halting notes and the third one
is sour. Nobody laughs at it twice.

## Shots (3 · est. 11.5s with margin)

**Shot 1 — the bustle** *(business 1.5 + reaction 1.2)*
Medium-wide of the workshop, honey lamplight, all five at their locked scales.
Ordinary noise: Peggi mid-clamber onto a stool she's been told about before;
Albert noting something in the notebook without looking up; Lily half-way
through tidying what Peggi has just untidied; Rose by the shelves, already
turning toward the door before the bell rings — she senses it first. The shop
bell rings once.

**Shot 2 — the box is wound** *(business 1.5 + reveal 1.5)*
Closer on the workbench: Richard sets the walnut music box down, both hands
either side of it — the apron-moment, head slightly bowed. He winds the brass
key twice and lifts his hands away. The box plays: three halting notes, the
third one wrong — sour, wounded, unmistakable. <halting music-box notes, the
third off-key> The lid's faded rose marquetry catches the lamplight.

**Shot 3 — the room stills** *(turn 2.0 + hold 2.0)*
Wide again. The stillness arrives in canon order, one child at a time: Rose
first (already still), Albert's pencil stops mid-word, Lily straightens, and
Peggi last — halting mid-blurt with her mouth still open. Four faces turn to
the box at four different speeds. Richard, without looking up, quiet — the
Richard law, quieter when it matters — asks the object itself:
{Now then… what happened to you?}
Hold the whole room for two full seconds after the line. End state: five
figures still around the lit workbench, the broken box open at centre, the
oak door dormant in the rear shadow — a describable frame, and beat 2's
first frame.
