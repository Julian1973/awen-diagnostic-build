## Framing
A chest-up single on Tom framed slightly left of centre, the near edge of the counter and the open box across the bottom corner of frame, the shop falling away warm and out of focus behind him. The surface directly in front of him at the bottom of frame IS the shop counter itself, seen from the customer side. There is no table, no second counter and no other display surface anywhere in this shot.
Screen direction: the customer side of the counter is camera-LEFT and the shopkeeper's side is camera-RIGHT. Tom and the children face screen-right; Richard faces screen-left.

## Style
Hand-drawn 2D animation. Warm coloured-ink linework in browns, ambers and muted greens, never black outlines. Flat colour fills over a subtle paper texture. A soft amber palette lit by honeyed lamplight, cosy and storybook, with a quiet melancholy running underneath it. No 3D rendering, no photorealism, no painterly brushwork.

## Do not
No motion blur and no action lines — this is a held frame. Tom has not started speaking yet — the mouth is closed and about to open. No text, lettering, signage or watermark anywhere in the image. No 3D render, no photorealism, no painterly brushwork. No extra people beyond those named above.
