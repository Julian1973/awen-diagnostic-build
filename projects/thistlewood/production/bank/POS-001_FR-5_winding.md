**2026-08-13. The first thing this studio has made and kept.**

minimax H3 · 768P · **9s** · one keyframe (SETUP-B) · emission **1,552 chars** ·

## Verdict
**APPROVED by Julian.**

## Why it worked, in order of importance
1. **No faces.** Nothing for identity to drift on. NEG-001's hypothesis holds:
   the constraint is character identity under motion, not duration. A 9s shot
   with zero faces is safe where a 15s shot with five was not.
2. **Locked camera.** The one move in the frame is the thing the shot is about.
3. **One clock, fully specified.** Setup (take the key), impact (two full turns),
   recovery (hand withdraws), button (the cylinder turns on alone). Nothing else
   competes for the nine seconds.
4. **Physics, not adverbs.** "Between thumb and forefinger", "two full slow
   turns", "the wrist rotates twice, each turn stopping and restarting". A model
   can photograph all of that. It cannot photograph "winds it carefully".
5. **Gated first.** 10.0 before a penny moved. Every earlier failure today was
   fired ungated or scored after the fact.

## The reusable shape
**An object-only insert with a locked camera is the safest shot this studio can
buy, and it should be used deliberately rather than as a leftover.** Act Two is
full of them: the compass needle, the crate of identical cams, Rose lifting the
crooked one to the light, the clog jamming the spokes. Those are cheap, safe
and, on this evidence, good.

## Cost of the whole lesson
Three renders. NEG-001 (15s, five characters, failed), the morning A/B pair, and
this. The negative was worth more than the positive: it produced the motion
budget, and the motion budget produced this shot's design.
